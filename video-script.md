# Video script — Labour CESS Tracking & Monitoring System

**Built from:** the live deck code (`src/scenes/`*, `src/components/*`, `src/App.tsx`, `src/lib/deck.ts`). Replaces the older `video-speech-notes.md` and `speaker-notes.md`.
**Audience:** Government of Karnataka · KBOCWWB.
**Running example:** ABC Commercial Complex, Bengaluru.
**Deck length:** 7 scenes · 31 beats (Space presses).
**Estimated runtime:** about 12 minutes at a calm pace.

## How to read this script

- **ON SCREEN** — what the viewer sees on that beat. Use it for the cut and the visuals. Do not read it aloud.
- **VOICEOVER** — read this aloud. Short sentences. Pause at each line break.
- **[Space]** — advance one beat.
- Say **potential exception**. Never say fraud or confirmed leakage.
- The figures on screen are examples for the story. They are not Board statistics.
